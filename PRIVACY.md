# Kavyl — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 18, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kavyl iOS app ("Kavyl", "the App", "we", "us") handles your
information. Kavyl is made by an independent developer, who acts as the data controller for the App.

It applies wherever you use the App, with particular attention to the EU/UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA,
Japan's APPI, Korea's PIPA, Australia's Privacy Act, and Singapore's PDPA.

**Contact for all privacy matters: [kitterive@qq.com](mailto:kitterive@qq.com)**

---

## 1. The short version

- Kavyl is a mood and emotion journal with a supportive AI assistant. **It is not a medical or
  psychological service, and the assistant is not a human or a therapist.**
- Your AI conversations are generated by **Apple**, on your device or in Apple's Private Cloud
  Compute. **We do not use any third-party AI provider** (no OpenAI, no external LLM company).
- We **do not sell** your data, **never** run ads, and **never** track you across other apps or
  websites. We do not use your conversations or memories to train AI models.
- Features such as battery/thermal awareness, the daily small task, and memory search run
  **entirely on your device** and are never uploaded.
- All images you add or create — avatar, greeting cards, and generated moments — are
  stored **privately**; only your signed-in account can access them.
- You can **export or delete everything** in the App at any time: **Me → Data & Privacy**.
- You do not need a phone number or an email address to use Kavyl.

### 2.2 Information collected with your permission

  health-related question**, so the answer can be grounded in it. That snapshot is used on your

  Health app or in **Me → Data & Privacy → Apple Health**.
- **Location** — when you ask something location-based (for example "what's nearby"), your location
  is used **on your device** with Apple Maps. The resulting place names, categories and distances
  (and coordinates, so you can tap to navigate) may be included in the prompt processed by Apple.
  **We do not store your location on our servers.** Disable anytime in Settings → Privacy →
  Location Services.
- **Microphone and speech** — only if you use voice input. Speech recognition and text-to-speech are
  handled by Apple's system services **on your device**; we never receive or store your audio.
- **Photo library** — when you pick a photo to attach, or save a generated image. See §4 for the
  images that are stored in our cloud.
- **Notifications** — if you enable them, we store a push token so notifications can be delivered.

### 2.5 What we never do

- We do **not** require a phone number or email address to use the App.

## 3. How the AI assistant works

To generate a reply, the App builds a prompt containing your most recent message, recent conversation
history, memories, your recent mood, and — only when relevant — the health snapshot, nearby place
names, your nickname, and on-device ambient context (battery/thermal). **That prompt is processed
only by Apple:**

- **On-device (default)** — replies are generated on your device with Apple Foundation Models.
  Nothing leaves your device.
- **Enhanced mode (iOS 27 and later)** — when a request is too complex for the on-device model, it
  may be processed by **Apple's Private Cloud Compute (PCC)**. PCC runs inside Apple's security
  boundary; **we cannot access your content**, and Apple does not use it for any purpose other than
  fulfilling your request. Enhanced mode is optional and can be turned off.
- **Image generation** uses Apple's image features (Apple Intelligence) where available.
- **Speech** recognition and synthesis use Apple's on-device system services.

**We do not use any third-party large language model service, and we never transmit your
conversations to a third-party AI company.**

## 4. Where your data is stored, and who processes it

| Processor | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (Supabase Inc., USA) | Database, authentication, file storage and server-side functions for Kavyl | Database hosted in the **ap-southeast-1 (Singapore)** region |
| **Apple** | App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, push notifications (APNs), Apple Maps, Foundation Models / Private Cloud Compute, on-device speech and image features | Per Apple's own infrastructure |

Your records are protected by **Row Level Security**, so your rows are only reachable by your
authenticated account.

**Images stored in our cloud.** Every image you choose or create in Kavyl — your **avatar**, your
**greeting cards**, and the illustrations generated from your conversations (**your moments**) — is
stored in a **private** storage bucket. Access is restricted to your own signed-in account by
per-user access rules on our storage, and images are delivered to the App through short-lived signed
links. (The built-in characters' artwork is a shared app asset, not personal data, and is readable to
signed-in accounts.) If you share a moment, we share the **image itself**, not an access link.

(Photos you attach in a chat are **not** uploaded — only a local reference is synced; the file stays
on your device, so a new device will not have it.)

## 5. Security

We use **TLS/HTTPS** for all traffic between the App and our servers, encryption at rest provided by
our infrastructure provider, row-level access controls, and least-privilege service keys on the
server. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so while we work to protect your
information we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active, and only as long as needed to provide the App,
comply with the law, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

You can delete at any time in the App: **Me → Data & Privacy → Delete Account & Data**. This removes
your profile, conversations, messages, memories, mood logs, snapshots and stored images. Deletion is
immediate in the live service; residual copies may remain in provider backups for a short period
before being overwritten.

## 8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to **access**, **correct**, **delete**,
**export/port**, **object to** or **restrict** processing, and to **withdraw consent** at any time.
This includes rights under the EU/UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA (including the right not to be
discriminated against for exercising them), Japan's APPI, Korea's PIPA, Australia's Privacy Act and
Singapore's PDPA.

**How to exercise them:**

- **In the App (fastest)**: **Me → Data & Privacy → Export My Data** to get a machine-readable copy,
  or **Delete Account & Data** to remove everything. You can also view, edit or delete individual
  memories the assistant keeps.
- **By email**: write to **kitterive@qq.com**. We aim to respond within 30 days, or sooner where the
  law requires.

If you are in the EEA/UK you may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority; in
Korea, with the Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC).

## 9. Crisis support and AI transparency

- The assistant **is not a human**, is **not** a licensed therapist or medical professional, and
  **does not provide medical, psychological or crisis treatment**. Kavyl is a self-reflection tool,
  not healthcare.
- If the App detects language suggesting distress or self-harm, it may show supportive messages and
  point you to crisis resources. This detection runs **on your device**. If it triggers, we store
  only a timestamp (not the content) so we can send a follow-up check-in.
- **If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line
  immediately.** Examples: **988** (US) · **116 123** Samaritans (UK/IE) · **いのちの電話** (Japan) ·
  **1393** 자살예방상담전화 (Korea) · **Hello KESAN / 1599 9999** (Malaysia) · your local emergency number.
